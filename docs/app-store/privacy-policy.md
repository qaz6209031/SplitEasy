# SplitEasy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

SplitEasy helps groups of friends track shared expenses and settle up. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## What we collect
- **Account information:** the display name and email address you share when you sign in with Apple or Google, and the account identifier Apple or Google gives us. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive the relay address.
- **What you enter in the app:**
  - Groups you create or join, and who is in them.
  - Expenses: description, amount, who paid, and how the cost is split, including the item breakdown for Smart Split expenses.
  - Payments you mark as paid.
- **Smart Split usage:** a record of when you used Smart Split (your account ID and a timestamp), used only to limit how often the feature can be called.

We don't collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or analytics. There are no ads and no tracking.

## Smart Split and AI processing
Smart Split is optional, and we ask for your permission before its first use. When you use it:
- **What's sent:** the description you type, any receipt or image you attach, and the display names of your group's members.
- **Who receives it:** our server passes this to OpenRouter, which routes it to an AI model provider. They process it to return a list of items and prices.
- **What we keep:** we don't store the image or the raw description. Only the expense you review and save is kept.
- **Who controls the AI side:** OpenRouter and the model providers work under their own terms and privacy policies.

You can withdraw permission at any time in **Settings → AI data sharing** and keep adding expenses manually.

## How we use data
Only to run the app:
- signing you in;
- showing your groups, expenses and balances to the members of those groups;
- calculating who owes whom;
- reading expenses with Smart Split when you ask it to.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Who can see your data
- **Other members of your groups:** your display name, and the expenses and payments in those groups.
- **Service providers:** Supabase (database and authentication, United States), and for Smart Split only, OpenRouter and its model providers.

## Deleting your account
Go to **Settings → Delete Account**.
- Your sign-in is removed, and for Sign in with Apple, SplitEasy's access to your Apple ID is revoked.
- Expenses you shared stay in your groups, shown as "Deleted user", so other members' balances still add up. Your name and email are removed.
