# Notes for App Review

**Sign-in:** use **Sign in with Apple** (or Google) on the first screen. No demo account is needed; a new account starts with no groups.

**To try the app:**
1. Tap **+** (top right) → **Create Group**, and enter any name.
2. At the bottom of the group, tap **Smart Split**.
3. The first time, a sheet explains that Smart Split sends the description (and any attached image) to a third-party AI service (OpenRouter). Tap **Continue**. "Not Now" keeps the text and offers manual entry.
4. Enter for example:
   ```
   I got the burger 18
   fries 6
   tax 2
   ```
   Then tap **Smart Split**. Review the items, assign "fries" to yourself if asked, and tap **Save**.
5. **+** next to Smart Split adds an equal-split expense manually. The **Balances** tab shows simplified repayments with **Mark as Paid**.

**Multi-person features:** other people join with the group's 6-character invite code (shown on the group screen / Invite button). Balances and suggested repayments appear once a group has more than one member.

**Account deletion:** Settings (person icon, top left) → **Delete Account**. For Sign in with Apple accounts, the app asks Apple to confirm, then revokes the app's Apple token and deletes the account.

**AI consent:** can be reviewed or withdrawn in Settings → **AI data sharing**.

Smart Split responses can take up to ~30 seconds.
