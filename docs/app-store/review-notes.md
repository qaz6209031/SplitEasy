# Notes for App Review

**Sign-in:** use **Sign in with Apple** (or Google) on the first screen. No demo account is needed; a new account starts with no groups.

**To try the app:**
1. Tap **+** (top right) → **Create Group**, and enter any name.
2. Tap **Add Expense** at the bottom, enter a description and amount, and save. Add a couple more.
3. Open the **Balances** tab to see what each person owes or is owed.
4. Tap **Settle Up**. Expenses are locked and the app shows the fewest payments needed. With other members, tap **Mark as Paid** on each; when everyone is even the group shows **Settled**. **Reopen group** unlocks it again.

**Multi-person features:** other people join with the group's 6-character invite code (shown on the group screen and the Invite button). Repayments appear once a group has more than one member.

**Account deletion:** Settings (person icon, top left) → **Delete Account**. For Sign in with Apple accounts, the app asks Apple to confirm, then revokes the app's Apple token and deletes the account.
