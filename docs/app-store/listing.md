## Description
SplitEasy makes sharing costs with friends, roommates and travel buddies simple.

✨ **Smart Split**
Type what happened in your own words, like "I got the burger 18, Alex had pasta 20, wings 15 for everyone, tax 5, tip 10", or attach a receipt. Smart Split turns it into an itemized split you can review and edit before saving. Tax, tip, fees and discounts are shared fairly, and totals always add up to the cent. It never guesses prices or people: anything it isn't sure about is left for you to fill in.

👥 **Groups**
Create a group for a trip, a house or a dinner and invite friends with a 6-character code.

💸 **Expenses your way**
Split equally in seconds, or itemize with Smart Split. Edit or delete anything later.

🔁 **Fewest payments to settle up**
SplitEasy simplifies everyone's debts, so the group needs the smallest number of payments to get even. Mark payments as paid and watch balances drop to zero.

🔒 **Private by design**
Sign in with Apple or Google. No ads, no tracking. Delete your account any time from Settings.

Amounts are in US dollars.
