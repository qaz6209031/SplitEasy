## Promotional text (≤170)
Track shared costs during the trip, then settle up once at the end with the fewest payments.

## Description
SplitEasy makes sharing costs with friends, roommates and travel buddies simple.

👥 **Groups**
Create a group for a trip, a house or a dinner and invite friends with a 6-character code.

💸 **Quick expenses**
Add who paid and who shared it; SplitEasy splits it equally to the cent. Edit or delete anything later.

🔁 **Settle up once, at the end**
When you're done, tap Settle Up. Expenses lock, and SplitEasy simplifies everyone's debts into the smallest number of payments. Mark each as paid and the group shows Settled.

🔒 **Private by design**
Sign in with Apple or Google. No ads, no tracking. Delete your account any time from Settings.

Amounts are in US dollars.
